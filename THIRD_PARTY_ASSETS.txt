Original generated material artwork added for v0.7.0

The scute, bark and leaf albedo images (xeno_scutes_v1.png, alien_bark_v1.png,
alien_leaf_v1.png) were created with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool.
These are generated original artwork, not photographs or photogrammetry scans.
They were integrated into the project's continuous creature meshes and scenery.
Prompts, source dimensions, file sizes and SHA256 hashes are in
docs/validation/cinematic-art-prompts.json. No proprietary game art was used.
The creature shader derives restrained surface relief from albedo in real time;
it does not use an authored displacement map or a generated normal-map set.

Original inhabited-world assets added for v0.8.0
